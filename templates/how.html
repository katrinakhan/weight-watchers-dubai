How this works

  1. Search by cuisine first (Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Indian, Filipino, Sri Lankan).
  2. Then search by restaurant name or area.
  3. Order the three plates: starter, soup or salad, main. Calories are approximate.
  4. Drinks matter: Arabic qahwa unsweetened, Chinese jasmine or green tea unsweetened, water everywhere. Skip juices, karak with sugar, lassi, boba.
  5. Read How to play it tonight on the restaurant page — that is the sequence for the table.

Sourced menus and AI first drafts

We do not copy-paste one Chinese meal onto every Chinese restaurant. Where we could open a published menu, the dishes are named as printed. Where we could not, the page is an AI first draft (yellow banner) so you can phone the kitchen and we can lock the real names. Calories are still estimates. Photos are typical plates, not stolen menu photography.

Seven per cuisine, then more when you call

Each cuisine starts with seven Dubai rooms — mix of mid-range and a few hotel classics. After you call and send a menu photo or PDF, we replace the draft with that kitchen’s real dishes.

Any restaurant not on the list

  • Starter: salad or soup, not fried pastry.
  • Main: grilled, steamed, tandoor, or sashimi.
  • Skip bread mountains, fryers, sweet drinks, and dessert if you already had starch.
