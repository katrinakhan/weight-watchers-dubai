How to use the app

  1. Search by cuisine first (Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Indian, Filipino, Sri Lankan).
  2. Then search by restaurant name or area.
  3. Order the three plates recommended by HN Fathima if you are watching your weight (starter, soup or salad, and main). Calories and macros are approximate.
  4. Drinks: unsweetened Arabic qahwa is one example — almost no calories. Water is always fine.
  5. Read How to play it tonight on the restaurant page — that is the sequence for the table.
